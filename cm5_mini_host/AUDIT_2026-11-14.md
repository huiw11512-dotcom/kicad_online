## Urgent review items

1. ERC: two root-sheet hierarchical labels `USB_C{USB}` cannot connect to a non-existent parent; also one un-driven power input and one un-driven unspecified module input.
2. DRC: 2 copper/board-edge violations and 2 courtyard overlaps. Verify whether deliberate connector cutouts/overhang were modeled using correct fabrication rules.
3. DRC: 6 annular-width failures at mechanical PTH pads with holes larger than copper rings. Validate whether these are meant to be NPTH locating pegs, not ordinary plated pads; do not blanket-suppress.
4. DRC: 1 overlapping same-priority copper zone; inspect net identity and polygon overlap.
5. 5 footprint parity issues including M1/M2/M3/M4 shield pins on J701; reconcile symbol/footprint pin numbers.
6. PCB has dangling via warnings and one isolated GND copper island; assess whether these affect continuity/return paths.
7. Numerous footprint library warnings are likely affected by incomplete library path setups and by the adapted KiCad version. Restore/validate library tables before making claims about their severity.
8. The high-speed impedance of PCIe and HDMI **has not been solved** against a specific PCB fabrication stackup and cannot be confirmed by length matching alone.

## Engineering release gates

Until corrected and separately reviewed, this imported baseline is suitable for exploring the PCB and testing our ChatGPT→GitHub→ZiroEDA workflow, not for direct manufacturing.
